Frequently asked questions

Can chemical drain cleaners damage my plumbing pipes?

Yes. Liquid chemical drain cleaners generate intense exothermic heat that can warp PVC drain pipes, soften glue joints, and accelerate corrosion in cast iron and galvanized steel pipes, while emitting toxic fumes. Professional mechanical snaking or hydro jetting is significantly safer and more effective.

What causes main sewer lines to back up in Las Vegas?

The most common culprits in Southern Nevada are aggressive tree roots (such as oleander, palm, or ficus roots seeking moisture), heavy cooking grease hardened by cold water, flushed wet wipes and paper towels, and bellied or sagging sewer lines caused by shifting desert soil.
