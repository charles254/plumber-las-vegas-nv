Why Copper Piping Fails Prematurely in Southern Nevada

The interaction of high total dissolved solids (TDS), elevated water velocity, and microscopic flux residues inside copper lines leads to pitting and formicary corrosion. Once a home experiences its first pinhole copper leak, additional leaks typically follow within months as pipe walls continue to thin. Upgrading to cross-linked polyethylene (PEX) eliminates metal corrosion entirely.
