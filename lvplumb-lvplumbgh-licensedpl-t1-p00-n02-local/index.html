Frequently asked questions

What should I do immediately if a pipe bursts in my Las Vegas home?

First, immediately locate and close your home's main water shutoff valve, typically found in a ground box near the street sidewalk or next to your exterior hose bibb on the front wall. Open an exterior hose faucet to relieve remaining line pressure, shut off electricity to flooded rooms at the main breaker, and contact our 24/7 emergency dispatch line.

How fast can an emergency plumber arrive at my Las Vegas home?

For critical emergencies involving active flooding, gas leaks, or sewer backups, our service technicians typically arrive within 45 to 90 minutes anywhere across the Las Vegas valley, including Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and Enterprise.
