Las Vegas Slab Leak Detection & Non-Invasive Foundation Pipe Repair

A slab leak occurs when pressurized copper water supply lines buried beneath your home's concrete foundation develop pinhole fractures or structural cracks. In the Las Vegas valley, shifting caliche clay soil, foundation settling, and mineral-induced copper erosion make slab leaks a common threat. Left undetected, sub-slab water leaks erode supporting soil, crack concrete slabs, and cause extensive interior property damage.

Frequently asked questions

What are the telltale warning signs of a slab leak in a Las Vegas home?

Key indicators include warm spots on carpet or tile floors, the faint sound of rushing water when all faucets are off, a spinning water meter dial, sudden spikes in your water bill, damp drywall along baseboards, or reduced hot water pressure.

Why is an overhead PEX reroute better than digging under the slab?

Copper pipes that develop one subterranean pinhole leak have typically suffered widespread interior corrosion and will often develop another leak elsewhere under the slab within months. An overhead PEX reroute permanently eliminates sub-slab plumbing vulnerabilities without destroying interior floors.
