High-Efficiency Ion-Exchange Water Softener Systems

Ion-exchange water softeners work by passing incoming municipal water through a pressurized resin bed loaded with sodium ions. The resin beads attract and trap hard calcium and magnesium mineral ions, releasing harmless sodium ions in their place. We install digital meter-controlled demand-regeneration softeners that monitor water usage, minimizing salt and water waste during regeneration cycles.

Multi-Stage Under-Sink Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Filtration

While water softeners excel at eliminating hardness minerals, they do not remove microscopic dissolved contaminants, heavy metals, chlorine taste, or total dissolved solids (TDS). Installing an under-sink reverse osmosis (RO) system forces tap water through a semi-permeable membrane that filters out up to 98% of contaminants, providing crisp, pure bottled-quality drinking water right at your kitchen sink.

  • Four and five-stage filtration including sediment, carbon block, and semi-permeable RO membranes
