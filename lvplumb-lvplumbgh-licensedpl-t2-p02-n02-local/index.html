Trenchless Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Structural Relining

Traditional sewer line replacement requires excavating massive trenches across paved driveways, stamped concrete patios, and established desert landscaping. Our trenchless CIPP process inserts an epoxy-saturated seamless felt sleeve into the damaged host pipe, inflating it with air pressure. Once cured, it forms a brand-new, jointless, root-proof structural pipe inside the old line.

  • No destructive trenching across driveways, sidewalks, or custom desert landscaping
  • Seamless epoxy pipe liner creating a 50+ year structural barrier against root penetration
  • Restores structural integrity and hydraulic flow velocity inside deteriorated cast iron and clay
  • Same-day installation minimizing household water shutoff and sewer downtime

Frequently asked questions

How does trenchless sewer pipe relining work?

A flexible felt liner saturated with specialized structural epoxy resin is pulled or blown into the damaged sewer line through an existing cleanout. It is then inflated to press tightly against the host pipe walls and allowed to cure, creating a brand-new, smooth, jointless pipe within a pipe.

How long do trenchless sewer liners last in Las Vegas?

Cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) epoxy liners are engineered to resist chemical corrosion, root intrusion, and ground movement, boasting an operational design lifespan of 50 years or more.
