Water Heater Repair, Installation & Tankless Descaling in Las Vegas NV

Reliable hot water is a daily necessity, but the extreme calcium hardness of Southern Nevada tap water makes water heaters the most failure-prone mechanical system in Clark County homes. As mineral scale precipitates out inside the tank, heating elements burn out, burner chambers overheat, and storage vessels develop catastrophic structural leaks. Diagnosing Heating Elements, Thermostats & Gas Control Valves

When an electric water heater stops producing hot water, mineral deposits have typically encased the lower immersion element, causing it to overheat and burn out. On natural gas units, faulty thermocouple sensors, electronic igniters, or malfunctioning gas control valves cause pilot outages and erratic water temperatures. We test electrical continuity, gas manifold pressures, and thermal safety switches to restore heating.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a water heater be flushed in Las Vegas?

Due to the extreme water hardness (280+ ppm), traditional tank water heaters in Las Vegas should be flushed at least once every 12 months, and tankless units must undergo a recirculating descaling flush annually to preserve heat exchanger efficiency and manufacturer warranty coverage.

Is an expansion tank required by code when replacing a water heater in Clark County?

Yes. Under current Clark County and City of Las Vegas plumbing codes, when a backflow preventer or pressure-reducing valve creates a closed plumbing system, a thermal expansion tank must be installed above the water heater to absorb thermal expansion pressure.
